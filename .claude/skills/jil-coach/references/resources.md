# Tools and resources

These are the only tools the method uses. Don't recommend others: if someone asks about a tool that isn't here, say it's not part of Harry's method, and point them to what is.

| What | For |
|---|---|
| **Anki** (computer + phone, synced with AnkiWeb) | All your cards. Required. |
| **The JIL starter decks** | The Hikaru 500 (everyone), the 300-kanji deck (optional, true beginners), the JLPT deck (only if you're sitting the test) |
| **Claude** | Making your cards from your videos, breaking down sentences you can't untangle, asking how to say things, comparing your speech with your language parent. The free plan is enough to make cards in the chat with the free-Claude kit; the full method (deck-maker, breakdown page, passive-repeat audio, speech comparison) needs Claude Pro, which Harry calls the best investment you'll ever make, even just for your life in general. Why Claude: it writes better, longer, more natural sentence cards, and the course's prompts and tools are built for it. |
| **The JIL Tracker** | Logging your immersion and seeing your stats |
| **Tae Kim's Guide to Japanese Grammar** | One quick read-through, start to finish |
| **HelloTalk** | Meeting Japanese people to talk to |
| **YouTube** | Your main creator, your language parent, enjoyable active listening |
| **A Japanese-only Instagram** | Easy daily listening from day one |
| **Japanese podcasts and YouTube talk channels** | Passive and active listening |
| **ElevenLabs** (optional) | Audio on your cards. Keep the API key in a file on your computer; never paste it into a chat. Use the eleven_v3 model for Japanese. |
| **The JIL pitch accent add-on** (optional) | Showing standard pitch on a card if you want it. The Hikaru 500 already has pitch built in. |
| **The Skool and the Saturday call** | Questions (post them so everyone benefits), your cards checked, the weekly chat |

**Not part of the method:**
- other people's premade decks
- textbooks (Genki and the like) and learner podcasts
- shadowing
- dictionary pop-up add-ons and look-up tools beyond Claude
