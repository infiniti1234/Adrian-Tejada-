# Creator search, 2026-11-12

**What you asked for:** a man's voice, standard or Kansai, calm and chatty. Interests: gaming, vlogs, chat or podcasts, challenges, and economy or internet news as a bonus. About 1,750 known words.

**How it was checked:**
- 30 Japanese YouTube searches found the candidates.
- Each channel's Videos tab was listed with yt-dlp to count its regular videos.
- Japanese subtitles were downloaded from 2 recent videos of 10 to 25 minutes per shortlisted channel.
- Speed is Japanese characters a minute, measured after collapsing the repeated lines in auto-subtitles.

**Video counts are minimums.** YouTube refused some page requests partway through a listing (HTTP 403), so a real total can be higher. It can't be lower.
